              Welcome to Rabbit Tracks

              Follow a question. Collect discoveries. See where your curiosity takes you.

              Rabbit Tracks is a research journal. It keeps the path you took from a question to what you know now, so you can always retrace it.

              1. Start with a question you want answered.
              2. Make it a trail. A trail is one line of research, built around that question.
              3. Save discoveries as you go. A Quick find is just a title and a link.
              4. Write a journal entry when something catches your attention: what it was, and what you learned.
              5. Keep following. Milestones at 1, 3, 5, and 10 discoveries mark how deep a trail goes.

              The trails you’ll see first are examples, there to show how a trail grows. Explore them, then start your own.

              Begin exploring takes a seven-stop tour. You can leave it at any time, or take it again from Settings.
